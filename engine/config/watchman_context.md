## What the numbers mean (established from his own data, Sep 2026)
- **HR up at fixed power / EF down after a break of ≥4 days** = plasma volume contracted + travel/sleep stress. Reverses in 2-3 days of easy riding. It is NOT fitness loss and NOT a reason to test FTP (a ramp test while fatigued reads falsely low).
- **rMSSD down + 1' HR-drop blunted (<30 on a quality day, baseline ~40)** = vagal (parasympathetic) suppression — sleep debt, travel, or a load spike on a rested system. Acute; resolves in days.
- **Very high rMSSD after rest (e.g. 60+ vs baseline ~30)** = post-rest supercompensation. He is NOT "ready to hammer"; loading hard onto it produces a crash within 48h.
- **Legs fail while HR/EF/HR-drop are normal** = muscular (DOMS window 24-72h after the first high-tension sessions post-break), not cardiovascular. Answer: easy days, not more rest and not more intensity.
- **TSB blind spot**: rest raises TSB but deconditions plasma volume/muscle. "Fresh by TSB" after a break ≠ ready. Re-entry rule: after ≥4 days off, first 3 days Z2/easy, first high-tension session on day 4+.

- Clean reps > crammed: if a rep degrades, lengthen the valley or drop 1% — never push a dirty interval.

## How to read a RIDE (debrief rules — learned 2026-09-19)
- **Outdoor vs indoor**: outdoor rides have surges and coasting; HR responds to the surges, so EF reads lower for the same NP. Use the OUTDOOR Z2 EF baseline (~1.43) for outdoor rides and the INDOOR one (~1.47) for trainer rides. Never call a 3-4% outdoor gap "fatigue".
- **Outdoor compliance**: judge power by NP against the planned band, not by "in-target %" (terrain makes that meaningless). Time and TSS overruns up to ~20% outdoors are normal (traffic, route, rolling home), not "oversized".
- **1' HR-drop** only means something after a hard final effort (quality days, IF ≥ 0.76). On Z2/endurance rides that end at soft pedal it is noise — never mention it.
- **Pw:HR drift**: use the MAIN-SET figure. Whole-ride drift is polluted by the warm-up/traffic start and cooldown. Main-set drift ≤3% = clean; 3-5% meh; >5% = fatigue/heat/dehydration.
- **Trend beats snapshot**: place the ride against the last 2-4 weeks and against CTL. Lower EF at CTL 43 than at CTL 63 in July is fitness lost to two breaks, not illness; it climbs back ~0.01-0.02/week on the ramp.
- **RPE and feel are Egor's own marks** (entered in intervals.icu after the ride: RPE 1-10, feel 1 = strong … 5 = weak). Read them against the objective numbers: RPE ≥7 on an IF ≤0.70 ride, or feel 4-5 on a day the power was fine, is the earliest fatigue signal we have and beats HR drift; feel 1-2 with normal HR confirms a good day. If they are missing, the ride was not yet rated — say nothing about them.
- **Test files** (T7 calibration, sensor checks, <20 min, TSS <10) are not workouts — never debrief them.
- "Felt strong + HR a bit high" after a rest day with high rMSSD = fresh legs on a de-trained engine. Both true; no action.
